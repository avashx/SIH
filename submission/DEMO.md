## What the demo covers

1. Dashboard on load — Sikkim with the five-class risk surface.
2. Filtering by risk category from the class rail.
3. **Click-to-inspect** — every conditioning-factor value at a chosen point.
4. Hotspot markers and the ranked hotspot table; opening one patch and reading its exposure
   attributes.
5. **"Sikkim only"** view — surrounding geography hidden.
6. Layer toggles across the nine conditioning factors.
7. Statistics panel — area, validation, exposure, cross-tabulations.
8. A closing statement of scope: susceptibility and rapid detection, not early warning.
